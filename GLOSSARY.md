# Office ordering

Shared language for colleagues coordinating coffee and bakery purchases.

## Language

**Office membership**:
A person's access to the private office group, separate from their account.

**Office administrator**:
An office member permitted to manage colleagues' office access and permissions, and cancel active runs.
_Avoid_: Superuser (when referring to this office role)

**Run**:
A trip announced by a colleague to collect items from both the coffee shop and the bakery for the office.
_Avoid_: Session, group order

**Runner**:
The colleague who announces a run and collects the requested items.

**Request**:
A colleague's selection of items to be collected on a run.
_Avoid_: Order (when referring to one colleague's contribution)

**Active run**:
A run that is accepting requests or in shopping. At most one run is active at a time; collection or cancellation ends it.

**Shop menu**:
The maintained selection of items offered by one of the office's shops.

**Menu maintainer**:
An office colleague designated to maintain the shop menus.

**Request line**:
An item selection within a colleague's request, with a quantity, a coffee choice where applicable, and an optional note.

**Coffee choice**:
The Blend or Specialty selection for a coffee item.

**Shopping list**:
The runner’s consolidated view of requested items, grouped by shop, item, coffee choice and identical note, with colleagues’ contributions available.

**Collected run**:
A run the runner has marked collected, ending its lifecycle and freeing the office to announce another run.

**Closing deadline**:
The instant at which a run stops accepting request submissions, changes and withdrawals.

**Shopping**:
The active phase after requests close, while the runner collects requested items or records unavailable quantities.

**Cancelled run**:
A run ended without completion, either by the runner or an office administrator, or because it had no requested items at its closing deadline.

**Unavailable quantity**:
The portion of a requested quantity the runner records as unobtainable on a particular run, preserved alongside the original request.


**Closing reminder**:
A reminder that a run will soon stop accepting requests, intended for a colleague who has not submitted a request.
